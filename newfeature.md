We already have a working HRMS platform that includes:

- Employee Management
- Attendance Tracking
- Task Management
- Project Management

Now, we want to scale this HRMS into a **multi-industry business operating system** that can serve sectors like:

- Hospitals & Clinics
- Restaurants & Food Businesses
- Temples & Religious Organizations
- Event Management Companies
- Schools & Educational Institutions
- Small & Medium Enterprises (SMEs)

The goal is NOT to build separate software for each industry, but to design a **single, highly configurable and modular platform** that adapts to different business needs.

---

### 🎯 Requirements:

1. **Core Platform Thinking**
   - Design a unified architecture where HRMS remains the foundation.
   - Show how additional modules (like inventory, asset management, bookings, etc.) can plug into the system.

2. **Industry Adaptability**
   - Explain how the same system can behave differently for:
     - Hospital (patients, medicines, staff shifts)
     - Restaurant (orders, kitchen, ingredients)
     - Temple (donations, events, volunteers)
     - Event Manager (clients, bookings, resources)

3. **Modular System Design**
   - Suggest modules like:
     - Inventory Management (dynamic for all industries)
     - Asset Management
     - CRM / Client Management
     - Booking / Scheduling System
     - Billing & Payments

   - Explain how modules can be enabled/disabled per organization.

4. **Dynamic Data Model**
   - Propose a flexible schema using:
     - Custom fields / attributes
     - Role-based workflows
     - Configurable item types (consumables, assets, services)

5. **Integration with Existing HRMS**
   - Show how:
     - Employees interact with inventory, tasks, and projects
     - Attendance links with productivity
     - Tasks link with inventory usage

6. **User Roles & Permissions**
   - Design role-based access:
     - Admin
     - Manager
     - Staff
     - Industry-specific roles (doctor, chef, priest, coordinator)

7. **Scalability & SaaS Model**
   - Multi-tenant architecture
   - Organization-based data isolation
   - Subscription-based module access

8. **AI Differentiation (Optional but Recommended)**
   - AI assistant per employee (chat + voice)
   - Smart insights:
     - Inventory predictions
     - Staff productivity
     - Business recommendations

---

### 💡 Expected Output:

- System architecture (high-level + modules)
- Database design approach (not full SQL, but structure)
- API design ideas (FastAPI-friendly)
- UI/UX flow (dashboard per industry)
- Key features that make this product unique compared to traditional HRMS tools

---

### 🚀 Goal:

Build a **"Business Operating System"** where HRMS is the core, and every industry can plug into it without needing separate software.

This system should be:

- Scalable
- Configurable
- Industry-agnostic
- Easy to onboard
- Powerful enough to replace multiple tools
